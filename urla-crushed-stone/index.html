Crushed Stone Supply for Projects in Urla and the Peninsula

Crushed stone supply in Urla is a local requirement, particularly for infrastructure, road, site preparation, concrete and fill projects along the peninsula. When planning a construction site in this area, an appropriate product size alone is not enough; delivery distance, scheduling and supply continuity are also important parts of the decision. With its position close to the coastal corridor, the BEMAŞ Urla site provides a regional production and supply option for these projects.

Crushed stone serves different functions across many projects. It can be used as an aggregate component in concrete production and can help prepare a load-bearing surface in road and infrastructure works. Suitable crushed stone gradations may also be considered for site fill, drainage, landscaping and temporary construction roads. The application must be clearly understood to select the right product.

Projects in Urla often involve different site conditions associated with tourism, housing, infrastructure, landscaping and the coastal corridor. A supply plan must therefore consider not only the quantity of material but also site access, delivery timing and the material's role in the application. Planning through a local facility helps clarify product and transportation decisions earlier.

Product and Delivery Planning at the Urla Facility

Depending on project requirements, the BEMAŞ Urla facility can assess product groups such as stone dust, fine aggregate and 5-12 mm and 12-22 mm crushed stone. Products should be selected according to the technical requirements and application. Aggregate for concrete is not selected using the same criteria as a product for road sub-base preparation or site fill. The project type and application should therefore be stated clearly in a quote request.

For projects in Urla and the surrounding area, delivery planning is often a sensitive part of the work programme. Construction site access, required tonnage, delivery time and product suitability should be assessed together. Because the BEMAŞ production network includes the Bornova facility as well as the Urla facility, a more balanced supply assessment can be made for projects across Izmir.

Requests for crushed stone in and around Urla are assessed according to the product definition, application and delivery location. Concrete, fill, temporary road and site preparation applications may require different product properties. This information helps clarify product and delivery options while preparing a quotation.

Project-Based Contact and Supply Process

The price or supply plan for crushed stone in Urla is not determined by a single fixed detail. The requested product size, approximate tonnage, delivery location, delivery time and application are assessed together. Accurate pricing and delivery planning are difficult without this information. Sharing these details when contacting BEMAŞ speeds up the quotation process.

Projects in Urla or along the Izmir peninsula can contact the BEMAŞ team for information about crushed stone and aggregate supply. If the requirement is for ongoing project supply rather than a one-time product delivery, a regular delivery plan can also be assessed. For project-based supply, contact BEMAŞ at info@bemasmining.com.

Using the same product definition for work that requires regular supply improves efficiency on site. When the product size, approximate quantity, construction site location and delivery frequency are provided in the initial request, product suitability and delivery logistics can be assessed more clearly. This can reduce material waiting times and make the programme more predictable for projects in and around Urla.
