What Is Road Sub-Base Material?

Road sub-base material is an aggregate-based product group used to prepare road formations and site roads. It can be considered to provide a uniform surface beneath the upper layers and support load transfer according to the project design. The appropriate product definition must be determined by the project specification and application conditions.

Road sub-base material can be used in applications such as road construction, factory roads, temporary construction site roads and platform preparation. The application type, product definition, approximate quantity and delivery location should be stated in the request.

For sub-base requests, the project description is as important as the product name. Concrete aggregate, crushed stone, fill material and road sub-base material may all be required at the same location, but each product serves a different purpose in the application. Through its Bornova and Urla facilities, BEMAŞ can assess these requests on a project basis according to the application, approximate quantity, location and work schedule.

Applications in Road and Infrastructure Projects

Road sub-base material can be considered for projects preparing for road pavement layers, internal haul roads, infrastructure routes and construction site platforms. In these applications, the aim is not simply to supply material but to plan the product according to the work programme. For road crews, supply continuity, product definition and site access are essential parts of the purchasing decision.

Road and infrastructure projects in Izmir can have different locations and work schedules. BEMAŞ can assess its Bornova and Urla sites according to the product request, delivery point, approximate quantity and project programme.

Road sub-base material can be considered for road layers and load-bearing platform preparation, while fill material can be used for more general site and ground preparation works. These products should not be assumed to be interchangeable; they must be selected according to the project specification and application conditions.

Aggregate- and Crushed-Stone-Based Supply Planning

Aggregate and crushed stone product families are assessed together when planning road sub-base material supply. However, the required size, expected mix and intended application must be clear from the outset. The technical definition, point of use and approximate quantity supplied by the project team help BEMAŞ direct the request to the correct product group.

The BEMAŞ product portfolio includes stone dust, fine aggregate, 5-12 mm and 12-22 mm crushed stone, fill stone, limestone, wall stone and riprap stone. Road sub-base requirements within this portfolio must be assessed according to their intended application. A request should therefore describe the scope of the road or platform application rather than stating only that material is required.

Supply planning for road projects in Izmir often progresses in stages. Preliminary preparation, layer placement, site crossings and ongoing vehicle traffic can create material requirements at different times. Providing BEMAŞ with a short summary of this sequence makes the quotation and production assessment clearer.

Information Required Before a Request

A road sub-base material request should include the project type, location, point of use, approximate quantity, product definition and work schedule. If the request is governed by a specification, the relevant product wording should also be provided. BEMAŞ can assess this information against the product family and facility plan and begin a project-based communication process.

A quotation request should state the project type, point of use, product definition, approximate quantity, location and work schedule. If a technical specification is available, the relevant section should be attached; price, delivery and product suitability should be confirmed against the project information.
