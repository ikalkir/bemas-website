Growing production capacity in mining

Active in the mining sector since its establishment, Bemaş serves with two quarry sites and crushing screening plants in Izmir Bornova and Urla.

Our primary goal is to reach a stable production level and quality standard, then evaluate the produced aggregate for export or within the Turkish market. We continue reserve development through new projects.

Controlled supply from quarry to project

Based on three shifts and 24-hour operation, our Bornova plant operates with 750 tons/hour and approximately 6,570,000 tons/year capacity, while our Urla plant operates with 600 tons/hour and approximately 5,256,000 tons/year capacity.

  • Quarry production, crushing and screening processes are managed in one flow.
  • Product groups are classified according to project requirements.
  • Bornova and Urla sites provide strong logistics access around Izmir.
