Crushed Stone Supply for Izmir Projects

Crushed stone is produced by processing stone through crushing and screening and preparing the resulting material for different construction applications. A project request is more useful when it identifies the intended application, requested grading, approximate quantity and work schedule, rather than giving only a general product name.

Crushed stone can be considered for concrete, road platforms, temporary construction roads, site preparation, drainage details and infrastructure works. These applications do not necessarily use the same product. Suitability should therefore be confirmed against the project specification, intended use and available product information before an order is agreed.

Through its Bornova and Urla production sites, BEMAŞ assesses crushed stone requests for projects across Izmir. The product requirement, approximate quantity, delivery location, site access and work schedule are considered together when discussing the request.

Crushed Stone, Aggregate and Stone Chippings

Aggregate is the broader material family used in construction applications. Crushed stone identifies stone products prepared through crushing and screening, while stone chippings commonly describe selected smaller graded fractions. Terminology can vary between projects, so the product should be defined by its intended use and specification rather than by name alone.

This page focuses on broad crushed stone requirements and the applications in which the material may be assessed. Requests for individual stone chipping sizes require a more specific discussion of the requested fraction, while aggregate requests may cover a wider family of coarse and fine products.

If procurement documents use several terms for the same requirement, share the exact wording from the project specification. BEMAŞ can then assess the request against the relevant product group and identify which technical details should be confirmed before quotation.

Application and Specification-Based Assessment

A crushed stone product discussed for a concrete application may have different grading and documentation expectations from a product considered for road work, drainage or site preparation. The intended application should be stated clearly, and any required technical characteristics should be checked against the project documents and product information.

Where a project specification is available, include the requested size or grading, application, testing or documentation expectations and any acceptance criteria in the initial enquiry. This helps avoid treating visually similar materials as interchangeable when the project requirements differ.

If the exact product is not yet known, provide the construction activity and the role the material will serve. These details give the BEMAŞ team a clearer basis for discussing an appropriate product family without assuming technical suitability in advance.

Planning with the Bornova and Urla Production Sites

The Bornova site can be assessed for requests from the city centre and surrounding districts, while the Urla site can be assessed for projects along the peninsula and western corridor. Which site may be considered depends on the requested product, quantity, delivery point and work schedule.

Site access, delivery conditions and the project programme should be shared during the supply discussion. These factors help frame the request, but product availability and delivery planning still need to be confirmed for the specific project and date.

Crushed stone may be requested together with aggregate, fill material or road sub-base material for the same project. Listing each requirement separately, with its intended use and approximate quantity, allows the product groups to be assessed without merging different technical needs.

Information Needed for a Quotation

A crushed stone enquiry should include the intended use, requested size or grading, approximate quantity, construction site location and target work schedule. If the project includes a product specification, test requirement or document request, attach or summarise it in the same enquiry.

To begin a project-based supply discussion, send a brief project summary to info@bemasmining.com. If crushed stone is required alongside aggregate, fill material or road sub-base material, list each item separately so its application and supply conditions can be assessed on their own terms.
