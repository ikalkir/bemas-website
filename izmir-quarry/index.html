Quarry-Sourced Production in Izmir

For quarry-sourced product requests, the material source, production site, product group and contact information are important. The intended use, approximate quantity and delivery location of the requested product should be stated for concrete, infrastructure, road and site preparation projects.

Through its Bornova and Urla production sites, BEMAŞ has a structure capable of responding to local supply requirements in Izmir. Quarry-sourced products include aggregate, stone chippings, crushed stone, stone dust, fill stone, wall stone and other stone products that can be assessed according to the project request. Because the product family is broad, each request should be considered together with its intended use and technical expectations.

When planning supply in Izmir, the facility location, product requirement, delivery conditions and work schedule are considered together. The Bornova site can be assessed for requests from the city centre and surrounding districts, while the Urla site can be assessed for project requests from the peninsula and western corridor.

Aggregate, Stone Chippings and Crushed Stone Focus

Quarry-sourced requests may cover crushed stone, aggregate, stone chippings, fill stone or road sub-base material. If the product name is not clear, the intended use, approximate quantity and construction site location can be provided; the BEMAŞ team assesses the request against its available product groups.

Aggregate describes the broader product family used in mixtures such as concrete and asphalt. Stone chippings and crushed stone are terms used for crushed stone products assessed in specific particle sizes. Fill and road sub-base material, meanwhile, identify the product's application more clearly. This distinction helps the procurement team communicate its request accurately and the BEMAŞ team direct the request to the appropriate product.

BEMAŞ product information pages provide application and request details for aggregate, stone chippings, crushed stone, fill material and road sub-base material. You can review the details under the product heading that most closely matches your requirement.

How Should a Project-Based Request Be Submitted?

A quarry-sourced product request should include the product name, approximate quantity, intended use, construction site location and work schedule. If the requirement is not yet clear, state whether the project relates to roads, concrete, infrastructure, fill or site preparation. This information helps the appropriate product family to be assessed.

To begin a project-based supply discussion with BEMAŞ, send a brief request note to info@bemasmining.com. If the product name is not clear, provide the intended use, approximate quantity, delivery location and target date. The request is assessed together with the relevant product groups.
