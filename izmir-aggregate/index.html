Aggregate Supply for Projects in Izmir

Aggregate is a core component of concrete and infrastructure projects. Sand, crushed stone and fine-grained products directly affect mix strength, ground behaviour, surface preparation and drainage performance. For aggregate supply in Izmir, product availability is only one part of the requirement. The selected material must also reach the project in the correct gradation and under a reliable delivery plan.

Through its Bornova and Urla production sites, BEMAŞ can plan project-based aggregate supply for ready-mix concrete plants, road works, infrastructure sites, site preparation and fill applications across Izmir. Local production sites make transport distances and operational planning easier to manage, which is particularly important for projects that require a continuous material flow.

An effective purchasing brief should describe the product, application and required delivery pattern together. Companies running concrete, road, infrastructure and site preparation works at the same time should separate requests by application instead of submitting one general material request. During the sales review, BEMAŞ helps identify the appropriate product group, the relevant production site and the site information required for a quotation. Include both the expected quantity and the intended application in your request.

Product Groups and Technical Applications

The BEMAŞ product portfolio includes stone dust, fine aggregate, 5-12 mm and 12-22 mm crushed stone, fill stone, limestone, wall stone and riprap stone. Particle distribution and material cleanliness affect concrete mix quality, while suitable gradation is important for bearing capacity and compaction performance in road and infrastructure works.

Companies seeking aggregate in Izmir should state the application clearly to speed up product selection. Aggregate intended for concrete may not have the same technical requirements as material used for site filling or road preparation. BEMAŞ therefore evaluates each request together with the product type, approximate quantity, delivery location and schedule.

The intended application matters as much as particle size. Concrete production prioritises mix performance, infrastructure works require suitable compaction, road platforms depend on bearing capacity, and site preparation may require effective drainage. Requests should therefore identify both the target application and, where available, the required gradation rather than referring only to aggregate in general.

Logistics Advantage of the Bornova and Urla Facilities

The Bornova facility is a strong supply point for concrete, infrastructure and construction projects along the central Izmir corridor. The Urla facility provides regional access for projects on the peninsula and coastal corridor. Working through two active facilities supports more balanced production and delivery planning for projects in different locations.

Communication is as important as capacity in aggregate supply. Site teams usually work to a project programme, so the material must be prepared in the correct quantity and at the required time. Sharing the gradation, approximate tonnage, delivery address, application and target date helps accelerate the quotation process. Contact the BEMAŞ team directly to discuss project-based aggregate supply.

Purchasing for medium and large projects across Izmir often includes multiple delivery points, different product sizes and changing site schedules. Evaluating the Bornova and Urla facilities together supports controlled material planning throughout the project, helping direct the right aggregate to the right application at the right time.

Include the quality requirements and any project specification with the product name when requesting a quote. This allows the BEMAŞ team to assess whether the material is suitable for concrete, asphalt, fill or road applications, supports faster purchasing decisions and reduces the risk of a material change during construction.
