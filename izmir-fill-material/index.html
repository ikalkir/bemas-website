Where Is Fill Material Used?

Fill material can be considered for applications such as construction site preparation, ground preparation, site levelling, areas around infrastructure, temporary roads and platform formation. The appropriate product must be determined according to the intended use and site conditions. BEMAŞ assesses requests within the scope of its Bornova and Urla production sites according to the project information.

The scope of a fill requirement varies by project. Material requested for site levelling at a residential construction site may not have the same requirements as material used around infrastructure. Drainage may be the priority in some projects, load-bearing ground preparation in others, and volume filling and surface levelling in others. The intended use should therefore be stated clearly when making a request.

BEMAŞ can assess fill material requests across Izmir within the scope of its Bornova and Urla production sites. When the expected product, approximate quantity, construction site location, intended use and work schedule are provided, product and delivery options can be considered more effectively.

Construction Site, Ground and Infrastructure Applications

Fill material can be considered for elevation adjustment, temporary haul roads, ground preparation around infrastructure lines, open-area levelling and changing fill requirements during a project. Road sub-base material, by contrast, is intended for more specific applications such as road layers and load-bearing platforms. Suitability must be confirmed according to the project requirements.

Urban and industrial projects around Bornova and site works along Urla and the peninsula corridor can have different logistics conditions. Which production site will be considered for the request is discussed according to the product, quantity, delivery location and work schedule.

Procurement teams looking for fill material often convey the product description using short terms received from the site. Expressions such as “fill stone”, “aggregate”, “crushed stone”, “stone chippings” or “material for site filling” may all be used in the same discussion. Stating where and for what purpose the material will be used when contacting BEMAŞ makes it easier to discuss the correct product group.

Aggregate-Based Fill Supply

Fill material can be related to aggregate and crushed stone product families. When the intended use is stated clearly, the product group, particle size and project requirements can be assessed. The relevant product information and quality documents can be reviewed before a quotation.

The BEMAŞ product portfolio includes stone dust, fine aggregate, 5-12 mm and 12-22 mm crushed stone, fill stone, limestone, wall stone and riprap stone. For a fill request, the application, approximate quantity, delivery location and work schedule should be provided.

BEMAŞ assesses each request on a project basis using product information from its Bornova and Urla sites. Price, delivery timing, product suitability and technical conditions can vary according to the project information; final conditions must be confirmed at the quotation stage.

Information to Provide Before a Request

A fill material request should include the intended use, approximate quantity, delivery location, site access information and target work schedule. If the product name is not clear, explain the current project stage and the purpose for which the material will be used. This information helps the BEMAŞ team assess the request within the relevant product family.

For project-based fill material supply, contact BEMAŞ at info@bemasmining.com. If your request also includes crushed stone, aggregate, stone chippings or road sub-base material, it is recommended that you mention this in the same project note. This allows the products to be considered more accurately within the same construction site plan rather than separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What applications is fill material used for?

Fill material can be considered for applications such as site levelling, ground preparation, areas around infrastructure, temporary roads and construction site platforms.

What information is required for a fill material request in Izmir?

The intended use, approximate quantity, construction site location, product expectations and work schedule should be provided for the request assessment.

Are fill material and road sub-base material the same?

Both requests can relate to aggregate-based products; however, fill material covers broader site and ground applications, while road sub-base material is intended for the road infrastructure layer.

For which areas of Izmir does BEMAŞ assess fill material supply?

Through its Bornova and Urla production sites, BEMAŞ assesses fill material requests for construction sites and infrastructure projects across Izmir on a project basis.
