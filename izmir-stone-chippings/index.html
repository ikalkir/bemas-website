Choosing Stone Chippings by Fraction and Application

Stone chippings should be identified by more than a general product name. The requested particle size, grading, intended application and project specification all affect product selection. A clear request helps distinguish smaller graded fractions from broader crushed stone or aggregate requirements.

Concrete, drainage, road, site preparation and fill applications can require different product characteristics. The intended use, requested fraction, approximate quantity and delivery location should therefore be provided before product suitability or supply planning is discussed.

BEMAŞ assesses requests for stone chippings through its Bornova and Urla production sites on a project basis. Product requirements, project location and target schedule are considered together, without assuming that similarly named materials are interchangeable.

5–12 mm and 12–22 mm Stone Chippings

The BEMAŞ product portfolio includes stone dust, fine aggregate, and 5–12 mm and 12–22 mm stone chippings or crushed stone. Each fraction should be assessed against its intended application and the technical requirements stated in the project documents.

Smaller graded fractions may be discussed for concrete, asphalt or surface-related work, while other crushed stone products may be considered for drainage, fill, road platforms or infrastructure. These examples do not establish suitability for a particular project; the required grading and acceptance criteria should be confirmed first.

This page focuses on selecting specific sizes and gradings of stone chippings. For a broader discussion of crushed stone applications, use the Izmir crushed stone page; for a wider family of coarse and fine materials, use the Izmir aggregate page.

Quality and Product Information for Selection

Product selection should account for grading, intended use and any quality or documentation requirements in the project specification. If testing, documentation or acceptance criteria apply, include them in the first enquiry so the relevant product information can be reviewed.

Products that look similar may serve different roles in concrete, asphalt, drainage or infrastructure work. Referring to the requested fraction and application together reduces ambiguity and gives procurement teams a clearer basis for comparing options.

Planning Requests with the Bornova and Urla Production Sites

Requests can be assessed through the Bornova or Urla production site according to the product, approximate quantity, project location and target schedule. Product availability and delivery planning still need to be confirmed for the specific project and date.

For a project-based quotation, provide the requested size or grading of stone chippings, intended application, approximate quantity, delivery location and target date. Site access information and any technical specification should also be included where relevant.

If the same project also requires fill material, road sub-base or another aggregate product, list each item separately. This keeps product selection and logistical details tied to the correct application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sizes of stone chippings can be assessed in Izmir?

BEMAŞ assesses project requirements for fractions such as stone dust, fine aggregate, and 5–12 mm and 12–22 mm stone chippings or crushed stone.

Can delivery planning be assessed through the Bornova and Urla production sites?

Yes. BEMAŞ can assess delivery planning through its Bornova and Urla production sites according to the project location, approximate quantity and product requirement.

Are stone chippings and crushed stone the same product?

In everyday usage, stone chippings commonly describe selected graded crushed stone aggregate. For technical selection, particle size, application and the project specification should be considered together.

What information is needed for a stone chippings quotation?

The requested fraction, approximate quantity, delivery location, application and target delivery date should be provided for quotation preparation.
